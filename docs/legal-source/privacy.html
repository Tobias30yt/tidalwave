12User Rights Under GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, and subject to its conditions and exceptions, you may have rights to:

  • Receive information about processing and access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data, request erasure, or restrict processing.
  • Receive eligible data in a portable format and object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on it, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Receive applicable safeguards concerning solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

These rights are not unconditional promises of deletion or portability. See the European Commission’s information for individuals.

TO BE COMPLETED
